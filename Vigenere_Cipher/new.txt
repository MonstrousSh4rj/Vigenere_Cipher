There is something deeply satisfying about the final stretch of a long walk. Your legs are tired and your mind has gone somewhere far away from wherever you started. The noise of the day has thinned out into something simpler, just footsteps and breathing and the occasional sound of a car passing by. You start to notice small things again, like the way light falls on a fence or how the air smells different near the end of the route than it did at the beginning. By the time you reach your door, you feel like you have earned something, even if you cannot quite explain what it is. It is not really about the distance covered. It is about the slow return to yourself that happens somewhere along the way.
